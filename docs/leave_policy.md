# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and new hires receive a pro-rated amount in their first year. Public holidays are not counted against the annual leave allowance.

Leave requests longer than three days should be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March, otherwise they expire. Carried-over days cannot be exchanged for pay while the employee is still employed.

## Leaving the Company
When an employee resigns or is dismissed, any unused leave that has accrued is paid out at the final salary rate. Leave taken in advance of accrual is deducted from the final payment. HR confirms the exact balance in the exit letter.

Notice periods are one month for employees with under three years of service and two months afterwards. Employees may agree a shorter notice period with their manager and HR in writing. Unused leave is normally taken during the notice period where the business allows it.

## Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year without a medical certificate for the first three days of any absence. Absences longer than three days require a certificate from a doctor. Employees should tell their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

Sick days do not reduce the annual leave allowance. Long-term illness is handled case by case with HR and occupational health.

## Parental Leave
Primary carers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave and secondary carers receive four weeks. Leave must begin within six months of the birth or adoption. Employees should inform HR at least eight weeks before the expected start date.
