# Onboarding Guide

## First Week Schedule
New hires receive their laptop and access badge on the first morning. The first week includes security training on day two and a product overview on day three. Lunch with the team is arranged on the first day.

Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy who meets with them daily during the first week. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and introduces the new hire to people in other teams. Buddies are volunteers and receive a small recognition bonus.

## Accounts and Access
IT creates the email account, chat account and VPN profile before the start date. New hires must activate multi-factor authentication within 48 hours of receiving their laptop. Accounts that are not activated within a week are suspended and must be re-requested.

Access to customer data is granted only after the security training has been completed and the quiz passed with at least 80 percent. Managers confirm the access level their new hire needs during the first week.

## Probation and Reviews
The probation period lasts six months. Managers hold a review conversation at the end of month one, month three and month six. Feedback from the buddy is included in the month one review.

A new hire who passes the six-month review becomes eligible for the remote work programme and for the annual training budget. A new hire who does not pass may have the probation extended by up to three months.

## Training Budget
Every employee has an annual training budget of $1,200. Courses above $500 need manager approval, and unused budget does not carry over to the next year. Conference tickets count against the training budget, but travel to the conference is claimed under the expense policy.
