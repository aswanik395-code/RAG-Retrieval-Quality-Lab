# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
The remote work programme lets employees split their week between home and the office, or work fully remotely where the role allows it. Employees may work remotely after completing their six-month probation period. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible until the plan has been closed by their manager and HR.

Roles that require physical presence, such as lab technicians and facilities staff, are excluded from the remote work programme. Employees in excluded roles may still request flexible start and finish times. Eligibility is reviewed once a year and can be withdrawn with a month of notice if the arrangement is not working.

## Core Hours and Availability
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their home time zone. Meetings should be scheduled inside these core hours wherever possible. Teams spread across several time zones agree their own overlap window with their manager.

Outside core hours, employees may choose their own schedule, provided they complete their contracted weekly hours and respond to urgent messages within two hours. Employees are expected to keep their calendar up to date so that colleagues can see when they are available.

## Home Office Equipment
The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset to every remote employee. Equipment is ordered by IT and delivered to the employee's home address, normally within ten days of approval. A one-time furniture stipend of $400 is available after the first year of remote work.

Equipment remains company property and must be kept in good working order. Employees should report faults to the IT helpdesk rather than attempting repairs. When an employee leaves the company, all equipment must be returned within five working days at the company's expense. Equipment that is not returned may be charged against the final salary payment.

## Working From Abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager approval. Longer stays require approval from HR because of tax and legal implications. Requests should be submitted at least a month before the planned departure.

Employees must not work from countries on the sanctions list maintained by the Legal team, and must use the company VPN at all times when abroad. Employees remain responsible for their own visa and work-permit requirements while working abroad.
