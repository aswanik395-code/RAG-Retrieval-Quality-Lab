# Office FAQ

## Office Facilities
The office opens at 07:30 and closes at 20:00 on weekdays. The kitchen is stocked every Monday and Thursday morning with fruit and coffee. Please label any food you keep in the shared fridge, because it is cleared every Friday afternoon.

Parking is available in the basement for employees who cycle or carpool; spaces are allocated by lottery each quarter. Bicycle storage and showers are on level B1. Lockers on level B1 are free to use but must be emptied at the end of each week.

Visitors must be registered at reception at least a day in advance and must wear a visitor badge at all times. Deliveries are accepted at reception between 09:00 and 16:00. Large deliveries should be announced to facilities staff so that the loading bay can be kept free.

The quiet room on the fourth floor can be booked for up to two hours and is not available for meetings. Phone booths on every floor cannot be booked and are available on a first-come basis. Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar system and released automatically if nobody checks in within ten minutes.

## Commuter Benefits
Employees who commute by public transport can claim up to $90 per month through the commuter benefit scheme. Claims are submitted with a monthly pass receipt. Cycle-to-work purchases are handled separately by the facilities team.
