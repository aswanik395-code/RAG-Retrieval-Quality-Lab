# Expense Policy

## Domestic Travel Allowances
Employees travelling within the country on company business are covered by the domestic travel rules. Trips must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least seven days in advance wherever possible, so that the negotiated airline and hotel rates apply. Employees travelling within the country receive a daily meal allowance of $45. The allowance covers breakfast, lunch and dinner and is paid per day of travel, not per meal.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in major cities and $120 per night elsewhere. Anything above these caps must be pre-approved by the department head before booking. Employees who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel cannot claim a lodging allowance.

## International Travel Allowances
Employees travelling abroad receive a daily meal allowance of $70 and a hotel cap of $250 per night. Allowances are paid in the local currency using the exchange rate on the day the trip begins. Employees are expected to book economy class for flights shorter than six hours.

International trips longer than ten days require approval from the Finance Director, in addition to the normal manager approval. The request should include the business case and an estimate of the total cost. Visa and vaccination costs are reimbursed in full when they are required for the trip.

## Client Meals and Entertainment
Client meals are reimbursable when a business purpose is recorded in the expense system. The record must name the client, the attendees and the reason for the meal. Client meals above $150 in total require written approval from a director before the expense is submitted. Approval requested after the meal is accepted only in exceptional cases.

Alcohol is reimbursable only at client meals and may not exceed 25 percent of the bill. Entertainment such as theatre or sports tickets always requires prior approval, regardless of cost. Gifts to clients are covered separately in the gifts policy and are not reimbursed through this process.

## Submitting Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted after 30 days are paid only if the Finance Director grants an exception in writing. Claims are normally paid in the next monthly payroll run.

Receipts are mandatory for any single item over $25. Missing receipts must be explained using the lost-receipt form, which needs manager sign-off. Repeated missing receipts may lead to the employee losing the right to claim small items without documentation.
